DanaLM (دانة)

A 62M-parameter customer-service model for the UAE, trained from scratch. It runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type leaves this page.

How it works. Your browser downloads the 4-bit ONNX model once (about 80 MB, then cached) and runs it with ONNX Runtime Web. The model writes a JSON answer, an intent and a short reply. It also scores all 21 intents; the chosen intent's share is the confidence. An answer is used on the device only if it is valid, its reply is in the customer's language and its confidence clears a threshold fixed on held-out real messages. Otherwise it goes to a person or a bigger model.

Masked message. Phone numbers, emails, Emirates IDs, card numbers and IBANs are masked before the model sees the text; only the masked text would ever leave a device.

Limits. English was checked on 64 real messages (84% intent accuracy). The Gulf Arabic, Arabizi and mixed parts have not been checked by a native speaker yet. Replies come from a small model and can be wrong. Do not enter real personal data.
